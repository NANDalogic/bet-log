Why TV can't get there

Even an antenna is ~19 s behind the field M. A feed built from TV can never beat TV, so cheap data services ($40–300/mo) land in the same 25–45 s range as Kalshi's free feed.

Hardware barely matters here

The window is 15,000 ms wide. Our home internet adds 67.

  • Kalshi's servers are in Amazon's Ohio data center (AWS us-east-2) M.
  • Free API tier = ~10 orders a second. Plenty.
  • Kalshi's market list (/markets) is cached up to 15 s; order books are live M.

What's in each setup
  • Stadium: ticket, phone with an order ready to send, fast thumb. Paid spotters could cover more games.
  • BetVision + tap: video 3–7 s behind, plus your reaction and the order trip ≈ 6–9 s E. Copy-protection (DRM) blocks screen-reading software, so a person taps.
  • Copy the tape: Kalshi's free WebSocket shows the first fast fill. Catches leftovers deeper in the book or on slower prop markets.
  • Institutional: stadium data under contract + server in Ohio + FIX + DoubleZero + $3–5k/mo odds feed + market-maker fee breaks.
  • Shared costs: server $0–65/mo (AWS c7i.large ≈ $65, a basic one $5–10), code free, Kalshi taker fee up to ~1.75¢ per contract.

The rules

  • ✓ Legal

    Courtsiding (relaying from the stadium)No US law bans it. Penalty in practice: ejection, lost tickets.

  • ✓ Stands

    A picked-off orderKalshi Rule 5.11: trader error generally isn't grounds to cancel the trade.

  • ◐ Unclear

    Kalshi's insider ruleDefines "insider" broadly. Nothing says seeing a public play faster counts.

  • ◐ Unclear

    CFTC (the federal regulator)Its insider theory needs a broken duty of confidentiality. A fan owes none, it appears.

  • ◐ Risky

    BetVision to trade KalshiLicensed for betting on that book; may break its terms (not verified).

  • ✕ Blocked

    Official NFL dataNFL has no prediction-market data deal. Genius Sports' Kalshi deal is soccer only.

    Kalshi sports in NV, WA, MICourt orders. Matters for stadium trading there.

Two things that would change everything

⚠ Kalshi speed bump

Since Dec 2025, Kalshi's rules (Rule 13.1) let it delay orders during live games. Not switched on for NFL as of Nov 6.

Crypto.com · 3 s delay for regular customersPolymarket · 1–3 s + cancels resting orders at kickoff

If Kalshi turns it on for everyone but market makers, the stadium and BetVision routes shrink or close.

NFL data deal

The NBA, MLB and NHL have prediction-market deals; the NFL doesn't. If it signs one, Kalshi's own feed could get fast and the stale-order window would shrink.
